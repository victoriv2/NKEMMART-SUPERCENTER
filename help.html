Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order on NKEMMART SUPERCENTER?
Ordering is straightforward: Browse through our categories or search for your product. Click "ADD TO CART" or "BUY NOW". Proceed to your Cart and click "CHECKOUT". Fill in your delivery details (State, LGA, Address) and choose your preferred payment method (Pay on Delivery, Bank Transfer, or Debit Card).
How long does delivery take nationwide?
Orders within Lagos and Abuja typically arrive within 24 to 48 business hours. Deliveries to other Nigerian states (South-East, South-South, North-Central, North-East, and North-West) take between 2 to 5 business days via our verified logistics network.
What payment methods are supported?
We support Pay on Delivery (cash/POS upon doorstep inspection in eligible locations), direct Instant Bank Transfer, and secure Debit Card payments (Mastercard, Visa, and Verve).
How do I track my order once placed?
You can track your order anytime on our Orders & Tracking Page. Simply enter your Order ID (e.g., NKM-782914) to view live progress through Order Placed, Confirmed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.
Are all products original and brand new?
Yes, 100%! All brand new products come directly from authorized brand distributors with genuine manufacturer warranties. For pre-owned electronics or verified vehicles, each item undergoes rigorous physical and diagnostic inspection by our quality control engineers before listing.

Returns & Refunds Policy

At NKEMMART SUPERCENTER, customer confidence is our top priority. We provide a 7-Day Hassle-Free Return Guarantee on all eligible items.

Eligible Reasons for Returns:
  • Product is damaged, defective, or malfunctioning upon unboxing.
  • Product received does not match the specifications, brand, or color ordered.
  • Missing components, accessories, or parts.

How to Initiate a Return:
1. Reach out to our customer care team via WhatsApp at 07042087633 or call 08155608447 within 7 days of package delivery.
2. Provide your Order ID and clear photos or a short video of the issue.
3. Our dispatch courier will pick up the item at no cost to you, or you can drop it off at the nearest NKEMMART Service Center.
4. Once inspected and verified, your replacement will be dispatched or your full refund credited to your bank account within 24 to 48 hours.

Order Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before it has been marked as "Dispatched / Out for Delivery".

To request immediate cancellation, contact our customer support team with your Order ID via telephone or WhatsApp. If you already made a prepayment via Bank Transfer or Card, a 100% refund will be initiated to your bank account immediately upon cancellation.
