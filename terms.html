You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and password.

All prices listed on NKEMMART are in Nigerian Naira (NGN). We strive for absolute accuracy in pricing and product specifications. An order is confirmed once payment has been verified or confirmed for Pay on Delivery. Delivery times provided are reasonable estimates based on logistics conditions across Nigeria. We deliver nationwide to residential and commercial addresses.

2. Privacy Notice & Data Protection

NKEMMART SUPERCENTER is committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and NDPR standards.

2.1 Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to process and fulfill your shopping orders, including:

  • Contact information: Name, phone number, email address.
  • Delivery address: Street address, landmark, LGA, and State.
  • Transaction history and saved preferences.

2.2 How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used strictly for:

  • Processing orders, delivering parcels, and handling customer service inquiries.
  • Sending transaction confirmations, tracking updates, and delivery alerts.
  • Preventing fraudulent orders and maintaining platform security.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any unauthorized third party.

We use cookies and browser local storage to improve your browsing and shopping experience on NKEMMART SUPERCENTER.

Cookies and local storage enable essential e-commerce functions such as remembering items in your Shopping Cart, keeping your account logged in across pages, and maintaining your Saved Items wish list.

We utilize aggregated, non-identifying telemetry to understand popular product searches and improve website speed and accessibility across mobile and desktop devices.
